04 · guidance

Technical, useful, local, alive

Accessibility is part of the identity.

Do

Keep green meaningful, pair network geometry with simple copy, preserve quiet backing areas for the logo, and label state as well as colouring it.

Don't

Do not turn every heading green, use cyberpunk type, add arbitrary logo colours, or rely on colour alone for device state.
